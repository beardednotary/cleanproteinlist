What "Clean" Means in This Comparison

When we say a protein powder is clean, we mean something specific — not marketing language like "natural" or "premium." For this guide, a product is considered clean for regular use if it meets all of these:

  • Lead: At or below California Prop 65 safe-harbor (0.5 µg/day), ideally <0.1 µg per serving or non-detect.
  • Other metals: Cadmium, arsenic, and mercury either not detected or well below health-based limits in third-party testing.
  • Independent testing: Data from Consumer Reports, Clean Label Project, or published COAs — not just a "we test everything" claim on the label.

Whey-based proteins consistently test cleaner than plant-based blends because cows filter metals before milk is produced, while plants absorb them directly from soil.

House Brand Showdown: Kirkland Signature vs. Member's Mark

If you're standing in either aisle, the obvious question is whether the store's own brand is any good. Here's the honest answer for both.

⚠️ Neither house brand has been independently tested for lead

Kirkland Signature Whey (Costco, powder) has never appeared in a Consumer Reports testing round — not October 2025, not the January 2026 follow-up — and Costco hasn't published independent data for it.

Member's Mark Protein Shake (Sam's Club, ready-to-drink) is a different format entirely — an ultra-filtered milk shake positioned as a cheaper alternative to Fairlife/Premier Protein — and it has no published independent heavy metal data either.

Neither of these means the products are contaminated. It means nobody has published a result either way, for either brand, and we're not going to invent one. An absence of data is not evidence of a problem — but it isn't evidence of safety either.

✅ The picks that do have a published result

At Costco, that's Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard or MuscleTech 100% Mass Gainer. At Sam's Club, that's ON Gold Standard again, Dymatize ISO-100, or Premier Protein RTD if you want a ready-to-drink option. All four have real, independently published numbers — which is more than either house brand can say.

Which Club Actually Wins?

Honestly? It's a wash on safety. Both clubs sell Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard and OWYN Pro Elite — the two most rigorously verified options in this entire comparison — at essentially the same price. Neither club's private-label protein has been tested, so neither gets credit there either.

Where they actually differ is selection, not safety:

  • Bulking or mass-gaining? Costco's MuscleTech and Dymatize Super Mass Gainer options give it the edge.
  • Want a whey isolate specifically? Sam's Club's Dymatize ISO-100 is the cleaner, more specific match.
  • Want ready-to-drink? Sam's Club's Premier Protein lineup is deeper and CR-verified; skip the untested Member's Mark shake.
  • Already have a membership at one? Don't switch clubs for protein powder alone — the verified national brands are the same either way.

The Real Rule

Buy the brand, not the store. Whichever club you already belong to almost certainly carries at least one independently verified option — you don't need to choose a warehouse club based on protein powder safety.

How to Check Any Tub (or Carton) in the Aisle

A quick 30-second workflow for either store:

1. Flip It Around

  • Look near the barcode or fine print for a Prop 65 warning.
  • If you see it: assume lead/cadmium is above the safe-harbor level and choose a different product.

2. Scan for Certifications

  • Good signs: NSF Certified for Sport, Informed Choice, Clean Label Project Purity Award.
  • These don't guarantee zero metals, but show the brand invests in external quality checks.

3. Be Extra Cautious With House Brands

  • Kirkland Signature and Member's Mark are both untested as of 2026 — treat them as unknowns, not automatic safe bets just because of the retailer's reputation.

4. Use Your Phone

  • Google: [brand + exact product name + "lead test"].
  • Or check CleanProteinList.com's lead-free database to see if the brand is listed as safe, mixed, or avoid.

FAQ: Costco vs. Sam's Club Protein Powder

Is Costco or Sam's Club protein powder safer?

It's effectively a tie. Both warehouse clubs carry the same top-tier, independently verified brands — Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard and OWYN Pro Elite are sold at both. The bigger factor is which specific product and brand you buy, not which club you shop at.

Does Sam's Club sell Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard?

Yes. Sam's Club carries Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard 100% Whey Protein Powder in bulk tubs, the same product line sold at Costco. It's Consumer Reports #5 (56% of CR's level of concern for lead) and Clean Label Project certified.

Has Member's Mark protein shake been tested for lead?

No published independent heavy metal testing exists for Member's Mark's protein shake as of 2026. It hasn't appeared in Consumer Reports or Clean Label Project testing rounds. That doesn't confirm it's unsafe — it means there's no third-party data either way, the same situation as Costco's Kirkland Signature Whey.

Is Kirkland Signature protein powder tested for heavy metals?

No. Kirkland Signature Whey has never been included in Consumer Reports' testing rounds or published Clean Label Project results. Costco has not released independent heavy metal data for it.

What's the safest protein powder to buy at Sam's Club?

Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard (Consumer Reports #5, dual-verified) and Dymatize 100% Whey Isolate (Clean Label Project Purity Award) are the two strongest verified options currently sold at Sam's Club. For a ready-to-drink option, Premier Protein shakes were Consumer Reports #6 in January 2026 testing.
