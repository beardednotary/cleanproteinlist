What About Creapure?

BulkSupplements is not a Creapure product. That is true, and for some shoppers it matters. But it needs context.

Creapure is a branded German raw material standard. It is useful, especially for people who specifically care about DCD and dihydrotriazine controls at the ingredient stage. But NSF Certified for Sport tests the finished product, which is the product you actually consume.

So the honest hierarchy is not "Creapure good, non-Creapure bad." It is closer to this:

  • Best case: Creapure plus strong finished-product testing.
  • Still excellent: Non-Creapure with NSF Certified for Sport, like BulkSupplements.
  • Much weaker: Uncertified creatine with vague purity claims.
