Is Kirkland Protein Powder Tested for Lead? Store Brands Compared

Published January 27, 2026 | Updated October 21, 2026 | 5 min read | By Ray Rothwell
⚖️

The Short Answer

Kirkland Signature (Costco), Elevation (Aldi) and Member’s Mark (Sam’s Club) have no published lead results. None was in either Consumer Reports round or in Clean Label Project’s 165-product protein study.

One store brand does have a number: Equate Whey (Walmart) tested at 0.27 µg per serving, 55% of CR’s level of concern, in CR’s January 2026 round. See what to buy at each store ↓

“Not tested” isn’t the same as “contaminated.” Store brands are made by contract manufacturers, and the retailer may well test them. But none of these retailers publishes results, so there’s no number to check against CR’s 0.5 µg/day level of concern.

Why the protein type matters more than the label

Consumer Reports found plant-based products averaged about nine times more lead than dairy-based ones, and most of the lowest results in its October round were whey-based. A store-brand whey is a lower-risk unknown than a store-brand plant protein. If you’re choosing between two untested products, that’s a reasonable tiebreaker. It isn’t a substitute for a test result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Has Kirkland Signature protein powder been tested for lead?

Not publicly. Costco hasn’t released heavy metal results for Kirkland Signature protein, and it wasn’t in Consumer Reports’ October 2025 round (23 products), its January 2026 round (5 powders), or Clean Label Project’s 165-product protein study. That doesn’t mean it’s contaminated. It means there’s no independent number either way.

Is Aldi Elevation protein powder tested for lead?

No published results exist for Aldi’s Elevation protein. It wasn’t in either Consumer Reports round or Clean Label Project’s protein study, and Aldi hasn’t released heavy metal data. Treat it as unknown.

Which store-brand protein powder has been tested for lead?

Equate Whey Protein Powder from Walmart. Consumer Reports tested the chocolate flavor in January 2026 and measured 0.27 µg of lead per serving, 55% of CR’s level of concern, and rated it better for daily use at up to about 1.75 servings a day. It’s the only retailer’s own brand with a published CR result.

What should I buy at Costco instead of Kirkland?

Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard 100% Whey is sold at Costco and Consumer Reports measured its chocolate powder at 56% of CR’s level of concern, rated better for daily use. Costco also carries Orgain Organic Protein, which CR measured at 143% of its level of concern (okay occasionally) and which is the subject of a 2026 class action.

What should I buy at Aldi or Sam’s Club instead?

Neither store’s own brand has published results. If you can shop elsewhere, Equate Whey at Walmart (0.27 µg, CR January 2026) is the closest store-brand equivalent with a real number, and Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard 100% Whey (56% of CR’s level of concern) is widely available.

The Bottom Line

Of the big four store brands, only Walmart’s Equate has a published lead result, and it’s a good one. Kirkland, Elevation and Member’s Mark are unknowns, not failures. If a number matters to you, buy Equate at Walmart or ON Gold Standard at Costco.
