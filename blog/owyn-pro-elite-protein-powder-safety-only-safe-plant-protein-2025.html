What "88% of CR's Level of Concern" Actually Means

📌 What "88%" Means

Consumer Reports measured OWYN at 88% of CR's level of concern for lead. Only one product in CR's entire 23-product round came back with lead not detected — MuscleTech 100% Mass Gainer. OWYN was not that product, but it still cleared CR's bar for daily use and was the cleanest plant-based option in the round.

CR's "level of concern" is 0.5 µg of lead per day. It's based on California's Prop 65 maximum allowable dose level, which CR uses because it's the most protective standard available. Important: CR states explicitly that no Prop 65 judgments can be made from its findings — the percentages are CR's own risk guidance, not a legal threshold.

So 88% means OWYN sits just under CR's bar, which is why CR placed it in the "Better Choices for Daily Consumption" tier with a limit of 1 serving per day.
