Why Mass Gainers Have the Widest Spread of Any Category

Two things compound in mass gainers that don't compound anywhere else.

1. Serving sizes are enormous

A standard protein scoop is ~30g. A mass gainer serving is often 300g or more — ten times the powder. If the raw ingredient carries even a little lead, a mass gainer serving delivers ten times as much of it. The contamination isn't necessarily worse per gram; you're just eating far more grams.

2. Plant protein is the multiplier

Plants pull heavy metals out of soil. Consumer Reports found whey averaged roughly 9× less lead than plant protein. Now combine that with a 300g serving:

Plant base × giant serving = Naked Nutrition Vegan Mass Gainer at 1,572%. It's not a fluke. It's arithmetic.

✅ The practical rule for mass gainers

If you need a mass gainer, buy a whey-based one. Every mass gainer Consumer Reports cleared for daily use — MuscleTech, Dymatize, Transparent Labs — is whey-based. The single plant-based gainer tested was the worst product in the study. That's not a coincidence, and it's the clearest signal in the entire dataset.

Wait — Do You Actually Need a Mass Gainer?

We're about to talk you out of a purchase, which is a strange thing for a site with affiliate links to do. But most people who land here shouldn't buy a mass gainer at all.

✅ A mass gainer makes sense if you:

  • Genuinely struggle to eat enough — you're trying and failing to hit a calorie surplus
  • Are a hardgainer with a fast metabolism and a physical job
  • Are an athlete in a deliberate bulking phase
  • Are recovering from illness or injury and need calorie density

❌ Buy regular protein instead if you:

  • Just want more protein — that's what whey protein is for, and it's cheaper per gram
  • Are trying to build muscle without gaining fat
  • Eat a normal amount of food already
  • Found this page because a mass gainer was ranked "safest" somewhere and assumed that meant "best protein powder"

⚠️ What you're actually buying

A typical mass gainer serving is 1,000–1,500 calories, and most of that is maltodextrin and sugar, not protein. MuscleTech 100% Mass Gainer is 60g protein and 1,500 calories. If you want 60g of protein, two scoops of ordinary whey gets you there for about 250 calories. Buying a mass gainer for general protein intake means eating ~1,250 unnecessary calories per serving, mostly as sugar.
